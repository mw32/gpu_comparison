Pick the H200 when

Bandwidth and scale decide it

Large-batch or long-context LLM serving, multi-GPU training, models that need NVLink, or any HPC job that needs FP64.

Pick the H20 when

Memory bandwidth matters more than compute

Decode-heavy LLM serving that is limited by memory reads, in markets where the H200 is not available. Expect roughly one seventh of the H200's FP16 compute, so prefill and training are far slower.

Pick the RTX PRO 6000 when

One GPU has to do a lot

Single-GPU inference, fine-tuning and development up to about 70B at FP8 or FP4, plus rendering, simulation and Omniverse, at a much lower price per GB.

Keep the RTX 6000 Ada when

You already own it

It handles 48 GB workloads at 300 W. For a new purchase, the Blackwell card has twice the memory, nearly twice the bandwidth and FP4.
